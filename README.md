# Power BI Reports

Six Power BI dashboards on sales, inventory and public data. Each report is available as a PDF; most also include the `.pbix` file to open it in Power BI Desktop.

| # | Report | What it shows | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual video game sales | Sales by platform, genre and region (North America, Europe, Japan, other) over the years | `.pbix`, `.pdf` |
| 2 | COVID-19 cases in Colombia | Positive cases by department, sex and age | `.pdf` |
| 3 | Sales 2021–2023 | Sales with filters by year, payment method, gender and age group, and sales by product category | `.pbix`, `.pdf` |
| 4 | Units sold by location | Quantity sold by country, city and quarter, with a map | `.pdf` |
| 5 | Inventory and sales | Inventory and sales by product model (bicycles and accessories) | `.pbix`, `.pdf` |
| 6 | Sales by region | Units sold by region, sales representative and quarter | `.pbix`, `.pdf` |

**How to view them:** open the PDFs directly on GitHub, or download the `.pbix` files and open them in Power BI Desktop.

---

### En español

Seis tableros de Power BI sobre ventas, inventario y datos públicos: ventas de videojuegos por plataforma, género y región; casos de COVID-19 en Colombia por departamento, sexo y edad; ventas 2021–2023 por método de pago, género, edad y categoría; unidades vendidas por país, ciudad y trimestre; inventario y ventas por modelo de producto, y ventas por región, representante y trimestre. Cada informe está en PDF y la mayoría incluye el archivo `.pbix`.
